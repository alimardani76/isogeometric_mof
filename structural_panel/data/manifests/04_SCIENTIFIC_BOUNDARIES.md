# Scientific boundaries for Figure 05

The structure panel is a **visual audit of matched chemistry under geometric control**. It is not an adsorption-site calculation and it is not a mechanistic simulation.

## Allowed / supported
- Show the two frozen structures in each matched pair.
- Show topology, chemical identity/formula, and the frozen case role.
- Show a local linker/node/motif inset when it is framed as a structural comparison.
- Show the geometry-control proof (`difference/caliper < 1`) as a compact QA device.
- Use the validated local-chemistry audit descriptively; panel B is the cleanest metal-node local comparison.
- Describe REPEAT-charge distribution distances as descriptive fingerprints/context only.

## Do not claim
- A specific adsorption site based on a VESTA image.
- A specific atom causes the adsorption difference.
- Oxidation state or charge transfer inferred from REPEAT charges.
- Directional substitution (A -> B) from the pair labels alone.
- A robust Cu-vs-Zn local mechanism for panel C; its local coordination assignment is method-sensitive.
- That the near-null panel is chemically inactive or has zero effect.
- A structural mechanism for the process-discordant panel without separate evidence.
- That the functional-motif panel represents a robust general class; it is explicitly exploratory.

## Special warning for panel C
Panel C is a **boundary case**. Keep the local view descriptive and visually matched, but do not use coordination-polyhedron styling or annotations that imply a uniquely validated local environment if that styling depends on a single neighbor-finding choice.
