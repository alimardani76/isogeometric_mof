# Final Figure 5 caption

**Structure-resolved examples spanning the matched-comparison support boundary.** Six non-overlapping framework pairs were fixed by predefined quantitative roles in the frozen matched catalogue before structure-resolved interpretation. Each panel compares endpoint i and ii as matched whole-framework views and as a paired local structural inset. The header reports the frozen median absolute log-adsorption separation, mean working-capacity (WC) concordance, mean selectivity concordance, and the maximum normalized geometry-difference fraction relative to the primary acceptance caliper (MAX GEO./CAL.). **(A)** Strong linker/process-aligned pcu pair, with a pronounced local linker-environment contrast. **(B)** Strong metal/process-aligned nbo Zn/Cu pair, retained as the cleanest local metal-node comparison. **(C)** nbo Cu/Zn pressure-exception boundary pair; the local ZnC4O8/CuC4O8 views are distance-defined and coordination-method sensitive, and are not interpreted as a robust local mechanism. **(D)** Near-null nbo linker comparator, showing that a real linker difference within the matched domain need not produce a large adsorption separation. **(E)** Process-discordant fsc linker pair; the local chemistry difference is shown descriptively and is not assigned as the cause of the discordant process response. **(F)** Exploratory sra functional-motif example showing a cyano-bearing local-environment contrast; the small functional-motif support does not justify a class-wide causal interpretation. Structural renderings provide selected-case chemistry context and are not adsorption-site maps or mechanistic assignments.
